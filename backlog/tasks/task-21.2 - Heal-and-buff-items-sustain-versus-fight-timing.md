## Description

Mekansm, Guardian Greaves, Holy Locket, Urn of Shadows and Spirit Vessel, Solar Crest, Pavise, Drum of Endurance, Boots of Bearing. Humans use heals when several allies are hurt or when a fight turns, Urn and Vessel charges on kills or to finish or deny heals, Solar Crest and Pavise on the core carrying the fight, and Drum and Bearing to start or escape fights. Mekansm, for example, fires as soon as one ally in 1200 range is under 45% HP with an enemy near.
