## Description

Heroes on the WeakHeroes list in bots/hero_selection.lua are capped to one per team because bots play them badly. Each gets a deep dive (hero improvement playbook, doc-2) and a lobby test. Done when every hero on the list has either left it or has a recorded reason and exit criterion (TASK-16).
