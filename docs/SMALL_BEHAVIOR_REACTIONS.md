# Nightmare saves, Chakra Magic, TP interrupts and neutral camp sharing

These changes are tracked in TASK-40. Automated tests exercise the policy with engine stubs; the lobby scenarios below still need real-game validation.

## Mechanics and references

- [Valve's Bane data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=3) explains that a direct attack transfers Nightmare to the attacker, and its first second grants invulnerability. This is a support sacrifice to free a core, rather than a dispel. The bot waits until the core is attackable, checks the modifier's source is an enemy, and only transfers sleep off positions 1–3 so supports do not bounce it among themselves. It prefers position 1, then 2, then 3, within attack range plus 250 units, when enough sleep remains to reach the ally.
- [Valve's Keeper of the Light data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=90) confirms zero mana cost, mana restoration, reduction of current basic-ability cooldowns, a self multiplier, and the strong-dispel talent. All values are read from the live ability. [The KOTL guide](https://dota2.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_of_the_Light/Guide) highlights replenishing spell users and enabling repeated disables. Chakra runs before ordinary offensive spells, scores useful restored mana and basic cooldown seconds, favors low-mana allies and cores in lane, and weights active fighting or retreating heroes. It excludes itself, ultimates, hidden/passive abilities, illusions and invulnerable targets. It avoids spending a cooldown on less than 50 restored mana (or half the ability's restore at very low values) without another useful benefit.
- [Valve's root-mechanics update](https://www.dota2.com/720) distinguishes teleport interruption from other channel interruption. This implementation uses an explicit list of reliable active disables, not inferred slow/silence/stun names. For a visible channel with modifier_teleporting, it checks current range, cast point, known projectile speed/delay, debuff immunity and unit-target spell block/reflection. Basic disables are preferred to ultimates. Point spells aim at the channeler's current location; no-target spells require the target inside their radius. Unknown and conditional spells keep their existing hero logic. The generic reaction never cancels the bot's own channel, spell animation or queued actions.
- [Upstream PR #166](https://github.com/forest0xia/dota2bot-OpenHyperAI/pull/166) and [issue #105](https://github.com/forest0xia/dota2bot-OpenHyperAI/issues/105) describe human camp theft in farm and roaming paths. The shared selector now checks distance to the resource, including humans without a bot farming mode. A human within 700 units conservatively reserves the camp. Another real allied bot within 700 units reserves it when farming or targeting a neutral there. An existing attacker retains the camp over an arriving idle farmer; simultaneous attackers resolve consistently by player ID. Dead heroes and illusions do not reserve camps.

Camp selection and actual neutral targeting both recheck ownership. Farm mode changes destinations even inside 400 units, clears the stale target, and does not remove an occupied camp from the shared spawn registry. Roaming opportunities and immediate/queued/pushed scripted hero attack orders also respect the shared check. Actual camp completion still updates availability normally. Farm spell area damage and engine-native orders are outside the scripted attack-order guard.

## Lobby scenarios

1. In both lanes, have enemy Bane Nightmare the carry with a bot support nearby. After initial invulnerability, confirm the support attacks the carry and takes the sleep. Repeat with an offlaner, with multiple sleeping cores, with a sleeping support, and with an allied Bane saving a core. Confirm no support-to-support transfers, allied-save cancellation, or interruption of the support's own TP/channel.
2. Give KOTL zero mana and ready Chakra: confirm self restoration. Put a low-mana carry beside a moderately depleted KOTL: confirm the carry wins. Repeat with a basic spell on cooldown, an ultimate alone on cooldown, a full-mana team, and the strong-dispel talent on a disabled ally. Confirm Chakra is not starved by Illuminate and does not cancel an existing Illuminate channel.
3. Have enemies TP while visible in range of Lion/Ogre, Sven, Lina and Centaur. Confirm unit, projectile, delayed point and no-target interrupts. Repeat at the range boundary, shortly before channel completion, under Linken's/Lotus, and under BKB. Confirm a piercing disable such as Primal Roar remains available and KOTL Will-O-Wisp is not used as a fake interrupt. Repeat while the bot itself channels or has queued spells.
4. Have a human farm a camp while a bot approaches, including the human more than 700 units from the bot but within 700 of the camp. Repeat with two bots, when the approaching bot is already inside the camp, during attack gaps, and from a roaming last-hit opportunity. Confirm the bot chooses another camp, the existing farmer keeps attacking, and the occupied camp becomes available when the human leaves. Check pulled neutrals and nearby lane creeps as well.
